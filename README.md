# 📚 Arabic MSA Resources

Welcome to the **Arabic MSA (Modern Standard Arabic) Resources** repository — a curated collection of college-level Arabic learning materials designed to guide learners through the foundations of the Arabic language using authentic, classroom-tested resources.

---

## 🧠 What's Inside?

- ✅ **20+ Lesson Plans**  
  Structured, detailed plans that guide learners through the progression of Arabic language learning.

- 📖 **Books**  
  - Main textbook: *Alif Baa – Introduction to Arabic Letters and Sounds*  
  - Supplemental books including visual dictionaries and college-level side texts.

- 📝 **Written Exams**  
  Real written exams from a college Arabic course to test your understanding and prep for assessments.

---

## 🎯 Who Is This For?

- Students enrolled in or preparing for college Arabic courses
- Self-learners looking for structured Arabic curriculum
- Instructors seeking ready-made classroom resources
